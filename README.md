## The talk

A lightning talk delivered at **Frontier Creatives**, hosted at **Pioneer Square Labs** in Seattle on **June 9, 2026**, for a room of designers, builders, and founders working at the edge of AI and creative practice.

The talk traces a single thread: software used to take a full cross-functional team to reach an audience, and now one person can do it. But that power is a trap if you point it in the wrong direction. Vibe-coding a polished "solution" on day one feels like progress while skipping the only question that matters, whether anyone needs it. The answer isn't new. Design has always been a loop, from the Double Diamond to the Stingray to today. What's new is that agentic AI compresses each turn of the loop from days to minutes. The talk closes with a live demonstration of **ProtoVibing**, taking a raw idea to a validated prototype in one sitting, and the mindset that makes it work: gardening, not architecture. Iterate, and throw away what doesn't work.
